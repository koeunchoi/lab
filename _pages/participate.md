### **Project BRAINY! (Lab Visit)**

![]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/respic/IRB-25-611_BRAINY-Flyer.png){: style="width: 400px; float: top; border: 50px"}

**Who**: Three-year-old children who speak English and their parents.

**What**: Three 60-minute visits to our lab on the Blacksburg campus of Virginia Tech

- Children will watch videos while wearing an EEG cap, ECG electrodes, and an eye-tracker, and play problem-solving games with our research team.
- Parents will complete a 30-minute survey about their child’s background, development, and media experience.
-	Participants will receive $90 gift card upon completion of the visits and survey.
-	Free parking is available for your lab visit. 

**How**: [Click to learn more](https://bit.ly/BRAINY_Interest) about our study and fill out our **interest form**.

**Questions?** Contact the researcher: [koeun@vt.edu](koeun@vt.edu) or [seyda24@vt.edu](seyda24@vt.edu) 

The Virginia Tech Institutional Review Board has approved this experiment for human subjects research (IRB # 25-611).
