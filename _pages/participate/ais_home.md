### **Let's Read with AI Reading Partner! (Home Visit)**

![]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/respic/Research_AIS1.png){: style="width: 200px; float: right; border: 50px"}

**Who**: Children ages 3 to 5 years who speak English, have not yet started kindergarten, and live in the New River Valley and surrounding areas of Virginia, USA, and their parents. 

**What**: 5 home-visit sessions (20 min each)
- During two sessions, children will play math and word games.
- During three sessions, they will **read a storybook** with our AI smart speaker.
- Parents will also complete a 10-minute online survey about their child’s background and media use.

**How**: [Click to learn more](https://virginiatech.questionpro.com/t/AVJAmZ1jnE) about our study and fill out our **interest form**.

**Questions?** Contact the researcher: [koeun@vt.edu](koeun@vt.edu) or [jisun@vt.edu](jisun@vt.edu) 

The Virginia Tech Institutional Review Board has approved this experiment for human subjects research (IRB # 22-357).
